## Community software

BranchRunner's Community product is source-available under Business Source
License 1.1. Savelkouls Ventures, LLC is the licensor. The existing Additional Use
Grant permits internal production self-hosting on infrastructure you control,
subject to the exact released LICENSE. Hosted or managed third-party services,
white-labelling, competing-product embedding, and proprietary redistribution
require commercial licensing under that grant.

Each version changes to GPL-3.0-or-later on its recorded Change Date. The policy
sets that date three years after the version's first public release. Before that
date, the BSL components are source-available rather than OSI-approved open source.

## Package and asset boundaries

The application, API/orchestrator, runtime worker, and agent wrapper use BSL 1.1.
The `@branchrunner/contracts` schema package is Apache-2.0. Third-party libraries,
fonts and icons retain their own licences and notices. Software licences do not
grant rights to the BranchRunner name, logo, or wordmark.

The release's `LICENSING.md`, package LICENSE files, and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
define the exact distribution. This page is a summary, not an additional grant.
Public pre.21/pre.22 artifacts are pre-releases; full release acceptance remains
open. Each keeps its original first-public date and conversion clock. The
current source files name version 0.1.0 and Change Date 2029-10-02. Both are
provisional until the licensor confirms the first public release version and
date. Use the exact versioned artifact's files rather than treating this page as
a replacement license. See [release status](/docs/release-status).

The CLA file states version 1.0 is in force from 2026-09-02 and also records
pending counsel review. Contribution intake status and trademark clearance
remain under review; this summary supplies neither approval nor new terms.
