## Community software

BranchRunner's Community product is source-available under Business Source
License 1.1. Savelkouls Ventures, LLC is the licensor. The existing Additional Use
Grant permits internal production self-hosting on infrastructure you control,
subject to the exact released LICENSE. Hosted or managed third-party services,
white-labelling, competing-product embedding, and proprietary redistribution
require commercial licensing under that grant.

Each version changes to GPL-3.0-or-later on its recorded Change Date. The policy
sets that date three years after the version's first public release. Before that
date, the BSL components are source-available rather than OSI-approved open source.

## Package and asset boundaries

The application, API/orchestrator, runtime worker, and agent wrapper use BSL 1.1.
The `@branchrunner/contracts` schema package is Apache-2.0. Third-party libraries,
fonts and icons retain their own licences and notices. Software licences do not
grant rights to the BranchRunner name, logo, or wordmark.

The release's `LICENSING.md`, package LICENSE files, and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
define the exact distribution. This page is a summary, not an additional grant.
Use the exact versioned artifact's files rather than treating this page as a
replacement license.

## Version and Change Date

The first public source release is **version 1.0.0**, and the BSL LICENSE files
name that version as the Licensed Work. 1.0.0 is a release candidate until its
release acceptance passes; see [release status](/docs/release-status).

The Change Date written in those files is 2029-10-01. The earlier `0.1.0-pre.21` and
`0.1.0-pre.22` pre-release artifacts are different versions: they keep their
own published parameters and original first-public dates.

The licensor keeps the current Additional Use Grant wording. Counsel review of
that wording is planned. Each released version is governed by the grant in its
own LICENSE files.

## Contributions and trademarks

The product's `CLA.md` is version 1.1. It names Savelkouls Ventures, LLC as the
party that receives contribution licences. External contributions are not
accepted until counsel has reviewed the CLA; see [contributing](/docs/contributing).
Security reports are welcome now.

Trademark clearance for the BranchRunner name follows later. The brand-use
policy in `LICENSING.md` is an in-house draft, not a trademark permission.

Use [branchrunner.com/licensing](https://branchrunner.com/licensing) to discuss a
commercial use case with the licensor, Savelkouls Ventures, LLC. Commercial terms require a separate
written agreement. This contact route does not offer a price, support commitment,
managed service, or licence by itself.
